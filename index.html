Dual-Spine Mechanics Principles

Dual Spines (Primary Backbone): Two continuous parallel longitudinal structural beams carry global bending moments ($M$) and shear forces ($V$). They provide total flexural rigidity ($EI$) and section modulus ($Z$) across the $8\text{m}$ span.

Perpendicular Deck Units (Flexible Skin): Short transverse deck slats lay perpendicularly across the spines. They act as a local load-distribution matrix, absorbing direct pressure and transferring it into the twin spines while bending locally under force.

Articulated Assembly Folding: The dual-spine framework and segmented deck slats allow the entire deck structure to articulate, contract, and scissor/fold for compact transport and modular deployment.

Euler-Bernoulli Beam Governing Equation:
EI (d⁴y / dx⁴) = q(x)
Where $E$ is Young's Modulus, $I$ is moment of inertia, and $q(x)$ is applied force distribution.
